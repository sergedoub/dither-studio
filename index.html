A small print laboratory.

Open or drop images, choose an algorithm, then tune the controls. Your files stay on this computer.

Scale: Dither DPI sets the sampling resolution relative to source DPI. Exports retain the original pixel dimensions and embed source DPI.

Color: Mono uses black and white. Tonal maps two or three inks. Indexed uses an editable palette of up to 64 colors. RGB quantizes each channel independently.

Export: PNG preserves transparency. Separations exports individual transparent ink PNGs and a composite in a ZIP. Soft edges are assigned to their closest ink. Batch exports every queued image with the current settings.

Independent implementation inspired by the public Dithertone Pro interface. Custom patterns, modulation, denoise, bleed and rounding are approximations. No Photoshop layer or video timeline integration. Animated inputs use their first frame; use numbered images for sequences.
